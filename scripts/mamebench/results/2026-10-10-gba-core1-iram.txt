Reading: with flash fetch mostly gone and core 1 idle 27% of the time, the remaining
~4.7 ms penalty looks like core 1's DATA traffic: it reads VRAM/OAM/palette and writes
lines into PSRAM through the same cache, and the display DMA too (nohash adds full-frame
sends and costs 0.7 ms). A next lever would be core 1 working from internal-RAM line
buffers and PSRAM writes in larger bursts, or rendering fewer lines per frame
(rendering stays on core 1).
