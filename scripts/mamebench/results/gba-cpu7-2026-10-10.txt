DYNAREC FOUND OFF (master code too):
- After the interrupted runs, Mario Kart came up on the interpreter (SPEED 74-79%, ~45 fps,
  "CPU core: interpreter (DynarecState)"), with DynarecState = 2 in its json. Cause: the crash
  guard. A launch sets TRYING; my runs ended with the console `launcher` reboot before 2 minutes,
  which sends no RG_EVENT_SHUTDOWN, so the next launch found TRYING and set OFF permanently. The
  alert was dismissed by the held A. A user who powers off or resets within 2 minutes of play
  gets the same result.
- The menu toggle cannot undo it in the same session: Off -> "On (next start)" saves OK, but
  xt_give_up stays 1 and the frame loop's `if (xt_give_up) ... if (jit_state != JIT_OFF)
  jit_state_set(JIT_OFF)` re-saves OFF on the next frame. I saw this on the board: after the
  toggle, the next launch was still the interpreter.
- Fixed on the card for Mario Kart only, by writing {"DynarecState": 0} to its json. After 135 s
  of play the json still reads 0.
- Also OFF on the card, NOT touched (cause unknown, possibly genuine): Teenage Mutant Ninja
  Turtles, Need for Speed - Underground, Super Mario 64 GBA (demo).
