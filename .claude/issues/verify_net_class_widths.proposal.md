# verify_net_class_widths — Proposal

**Status:** PROPOSE-ONLY. No `hardware/`, `release_jlcpcb/`,
`scripts/generate_pcb/` edits. Reproduce with
`python3 scripts/verify_net_class_widths.py`.

## 1. Root cause

The 4 undersized traces are the **VBUS parallel bond between USB-C
lands 2 and 11**, generated by `scripts/generate_pcb/routing.py`
lines **3555–3654** (function `route_usb_c_bypasses`).

The gate width is not a typo or an oversight; it is **solved from
the connector footprint** at line 3609:

    _esc_w = round(min(W_PWR_HIGH, _free - 2 * _MARGIN), 3)

with `_free` derived from the peg-to-land-10 span (line 3608), so it
always uses the whole available budget and can never exceed
`W_PWR_HIGH` (0.76 mm). Today `_free = 0.293 mm`, hence `_esc_w =
0.273 mm`.

The exact segments the gate emits (from `pcb_cache.load_cache()`,
key `"width"`):

| # | Net  | Layer | (x1, y1) mm         | (x2, y2) mm         | width mm | length mm |
|---|------|-------|---------------------|---------------------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | VBUS | B.Cu  | (77.6000, 68.7750)  | (77.5370, 68.9990)  | 0.273    | 0.233     |
| 2 | VBUS | B.Cu  | (82.4000, 68.7750)  | (82.4630, 68.9990)  | 0.273    | 0.233     |
| 3 | VBUS | B.Cu  | (77.5370, 68.9990)  | (78.1880, 70.2390)  | 0.273    | 1.401     |
| 4 | VBUS | B.Cu  | (81.8120, 70.2390)  | (82.4630, 68.9990)  | 0.273    | 1.401     |

Segments 1 and 2 are the "in-land stubs" that start at the pad
origin — pad 11 at (77.6, 68.775) and pad 2 at (82.4, 68.775) are
each 0.55 × 1.10 mm SMD lands, so those 0.23 mm-long spines lie
**wholly inside their own pad copper** (dy = 0.224 mm < pad half-
height 0.550 mm). They are not real necks; the underlying copper is
0.55 mm. Segments 3 and 4 are the real gates, one on each side, each
threading the diagonal between the outer peg NPTH and the corner of
adjacent land 10.

## 2. Why the gate is right — and why the neck is unavoidable

The peg-to-land-10 diagonal is fixed by the USB-C connector's own
datasheet dimensions (both features belong to J1). Along that line
the free copper budget is, per the routing comment lines 3567–3576:

    |PQ|  = 1.1183 mm       (peg centre → land-10 corner)
    - 0.325 mm  peg NPTH radius
    - 0.300 mm  NPTH-to-copper (validate_jlcpcb, stricter than the
                0.254 mm in the .kicad_dru → tighter one wins)
    - 0.200 mm  pad-to-track (land 10 side)
    = 0.293 mm  widest trace the geometry admits, before margin
    − 0.020 mm  KiCad polygon chord margin
    = 0.273 mm  ← the emitted width

Confirmed independently by a maximin-clearance search on a raster
clearance field (0.2888 mm at 5 µm resolution, pinch exactly at
(77.795, 69.485)). The "other" escape route — upward into the board
interior — measures 0.170 mm and is topologically blocked further
on by lands 3..10 dropping through the F.Cu button band. See
routing.py:3577–3583.

**No re-routing of this board can reach 0.50 mm at this gate.**
Widening requires either a different USB-C footprint (out of scope
for v1) or moving the peg holes/lands (they belong to the connector,
so out of scope for any board revision using this part).

## 3. Ampacity verdict — is it safe?

**Yes for the design case, with margin.** IPC-2221 external, 1 oz
Cu (35 µm), 10 °C rise gives 0.933 A for a 0.273 mm trace
(cross-section 14.81 mils², k = 0.048). Actual load at each gate:

- Parallel-path analysis (sheet resistance 0.48 mΩ/□, USB-C
  contact ~ 30 mΩ/pin):
  - pad 2 direct path (0.60 mm × ~8 mm + 0.76 mm × ~50 mm): ~37.6 mΩ
  - pad 11 via bond (2 × neck 2.87 mΩ + corridor 2.5 mΩ + pad 2
    trace 37.6 mΩ): ~75.8 mΩ end-to-end
  - Split at 2.1 A charging (IP5306 datasheet peak): pad 2 = 1.11 A,
    pad 11 = 0.99 A → **gate current ≈ 1.0 A**
- Temperature rise at 1.0 A on 0.273 mm × 35 µm: **≈ 11 °C** (IPC-
  2221 solved for ΔT). Below the 10 °C design line by a hair, well
  inside the 30 °C rise most designs tolerate.
- 3 A USB source (upper USB-C limit) split same way: gate carries
  ~1.41 A → **ΔT ≈ 24 °C**. Still safe.
- Only fault mode that stresses the gate is a totally failed
  A4/B9 (pad 2) contact forcing all 2.1 A through the bond →
  **ΔT ≈ 57 °C**. Not a normal operating case; a broken pin fails
  observably by slow charging first.

Neck length also matters: 1.40 mm of narrow copper between two wide
(0.76 mm) plateaus is a short thermal spike — steady-state current
does not fully develop at that length. The steady-state number above
is conservative.

## 4. Proposed fix — allowlist, do not widen

The routing.py author decided in R22 (2026-07-25, line 3585) to
**leave this gate red** as a permanent v2 flag. That predates the
"a red gate stops being read" feedback in `MEMORY.md`. Given that
feedback, the proposal is:

**Add a `USB_VBUS_GATE_ALLOWLIST` to `scripts/verify_net_class_widths.py`
that whitelists exactly these 4 segments**, with the same structure
and evidentiary standard as the existing `POWER_HIGH_ALLOWLIST` for
the BAT+ corridor.

Exact edit description (do not apply in this pass):

- In `scripts/verify_net_class_widths.py`, add a second allowlist
  after `POWER_HIGH_ALLOWLIST` (~line 91), with the same
  `(net, layer, x1, y1, x2, y2, width)` shape and tolerance:

    USB_VBUS_GATE_ALLOWLIST = [
        # J1 VBUS parallel-bond gate — geometrically unwidenable.
        # Proof: routing.py:3555-3615 (peg-to-land-10 free span
        # = 0.293mm, capped at 0.273mm with 0.02mm margin).
        # Ampacity: 0.93A at 10°C rise (IPC-2221). Design load
        # at 2.1A charging split ≈ 1.0A → ΔT ≈ 11°C. Neck length
        # 1.40mm. Not a supply path; land 2 keeps its own 0.60mm
        # run to the IP5306.
        # Segments 1-2 are in-land stubs (whole pad is 0.55mm),
        # segments 3-4 are the real diagonal escapes.
        ("VBUS", "B.Cu", 77.600, 68.775, 77.537, 68.999, 0.273),
        ("VBUS", "B.Cu", 82.400, 68.775, 82.463, 68.999, 0.273),
        ("VBUS", "B.Cu", 77.537, 68.999, 78.188, 70.239, 0.273),
        ("VBUS", "B.Cu", 81.812, 70.239, 82.463, 68.999, 0.273),
    ]

- In `test_net_class_widths()` around line 185, extend the Power
  High filter to also drop matches against `USB_VBUS_GATE_ALLOWLIST`,
  with a `ALLOWED:` print line mirroring the BAT+ one.

- Simultaneously, **soften** the routing.py comment at lines
  3584–3586 to point at the allowlist entry rather than instruct
  the verifier to keep failing.

Alternatives considered and rejected:

- **Widen to 0.293 mm** (the true geometric max): would still fail
  the 0.50 mm class minimum, gains only 0.02 mm of copper, and
  costs the KiCad polygon chord safety margin. Not worth it.
- **Change the "Power High" minimum to 0.30 mm**: would blind the
  gate to real regressions elsewhere on VBUS/BAT+/LX.
- **Split "Power High" into a "connector-gate" subclass**: heavier
  refactor for the same effect the allowlist gives.
- **Move to a different USB-C part**: v2 hardware change, out of
  scope for a verify-gate fix.

Explicit assumption for user review: this proposal treats the
"never silence errors" and "red gate stops being read" principles
in `MEMORY.md` as the tie-breaker over the routing.py author's
"deliberately NOT allowlisted" note. If the intent was to keep this
red as a permanent v2 marker, the correct answer is instead to
**move it out of `make verify-all`** and into a dedicated v2
tracking list — silencing it in the daily gate one way or another,
so real regressions on Power High are visible again.

## 5. Blast radius

- `scripts/verify_net_class_widths.py` (add allowlist + filter).
- `scripts/generate_pcb/routing.py` (docstring at 3584–3586,
  no code change, no PCB regeneration needed).
- **No** change to `hardware/kicad/esp32-emu-turbo.kicad_pcb`.
- **No** change to `release_jlcpcb/` (gerbers, BOM, CPL unchanged).
- **No** change to `software/main/board_config.h` (GPIO map untouched).
- **No** change to docs beyond a short note in
  `website/docs/verification.md` if that file enumerates the gates.
- `make verify-all` goes from 66-check red → 66-check green on this
  gate; the other current reds (`verify_netlist_diff`,
  `verify_dangling_copper`, floating SW15/SW14) are unaffected.
